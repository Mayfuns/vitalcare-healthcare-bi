# VitalCare Health Group — Healthcare Business Intelligence

A Power BI case study analysing hospital operations, clinical outcomes and financial performance across **2023 and 2024**.


## Dashboard pages

The Power BI report contains three pages:

1. [Healthcare Overview](01_dashboard/vitalcare-overview.png)
2. [Clinical and Patients Analysis](01_dashboard/vitalcare-clinical-analysis.png)
3. [Financial Analysis](01_dashboard/vitalcare-financial-analysis.png)

Each page can also be viewed directly through the portfolio's dashboard gallery.

## Business question

**How did hospital performance change from 2023 to 2024 across operations, clinical outcomes and financial performance, and which changes deserve further investigation?**

## Key findings

| KPI | Result | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Revenue | **+5.75% YoY** | Top-line financial performance improved in 2024. |
| Mortality | **−15.77% YoY** | This clinical outcome measure improved year on year. |
| Readmissions | **+8.68% YoY** | Readmissions increased and warrant further investigation. |
| Profit margin | **14.67% in 2024** | Margin declined slightly from **14.82%**, despite revenue growth. |

The combined view is important because no single KPI tells the full story. Revenue growth can be considered alongside cost and margin performance, while the improvement in mortality can be reviewed alongside the increase in readmissions.
